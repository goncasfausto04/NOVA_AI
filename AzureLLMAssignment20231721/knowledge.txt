## Collaboration and Partnership
Both Professor Vitor Santos and Marco António Silva collaborate extensively on AI education and community building in Portugal. They are co-hosts of the "Building The Future - AI Portugal Podcast," where they discuss cutting-edge AI topics, interview experts, and analyze how AI impacts different sectors including education, healthcare, technology, and business.

Professor Vitor Santos brings deep academic expertise with a PhD in Information Systems and over 20 years of teaching experience across multiple Portuguese universities, while Marco António Silva provides industry perspective from his extensive work at Microsoft in AI, cloud technologies, and innovation leadership. Together, they represent a powerful bridge between academic research and practical AI implementation in Portugal.

Both professors teach at NOVA IMS, making the institution a hub for AI education combining theoretical foundations with practical, industry-relevant applications using Microsoft Azure AI technologies.
